# GumaWeb

## Executive Summary

GumaWeb is an Autonomous Website Factory designed to identify businesses without a modern web presence, generate production-ready websites automatically, publish them under Guma-controlled infrastructure, and convert those websites into customer acquisition assets through a claim-first business model.

Unlike traditional website builders that require businesses to initiate website creation, GumaWeb creates websites first and acquires customers second.

Core Proposition:

> We already built your website.
> Claim it.

This significantly reduces adoption friction and creates a scalable lead-generation mechanism for Guma One.
