# Outreach Engine

## Objective

Transform generated websites into qualified claim opportunities.

The outreach engine is the customer acquisition layer of GumaWeb.

---

# Primary Goals

1. Notify businesses of their generated website.
2. Drive claim conversions.
3. Increase trust.
4. Nurture prospects toward premium plans.

---

# Outreach Architecture

```text
Website Published
        ↓
Contact Discovery
        ↓
Outreach Campaign
        ↓
Website Visit
        ↓
Claim Request
        ↓
Conversion Tracking
```
