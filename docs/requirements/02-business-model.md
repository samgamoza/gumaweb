# Business Model

## Traditional Model

Customer discovers need.

Customer builds website.

Customer publishes website.

```text
Need
 ↓
Purchase
 ↓
Build
 ↓
Launch
```

High friction.

---

## GumaWeb Model

```text
Directory Record
 ↓
Website Generated
 ↓
Site Published
 ↓
Outreach
 ↓
Customer Claims Site
 ↓
Customer Upgrades
```

Low friction acquisition.
